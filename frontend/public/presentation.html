Information Theory

Directional Shannon Entropy: The Precursor Metric

Detecting crowd turbulence and shockwaves before physical crush occurs

Why Entropy Detects What Headcount Misses

High density alone is not fatal if everyone moves in unison. Disasters occur when opposing human streams clash, creating circular eddies and turbulent disorder. By measuring angular motion distribution across 8 sectors (45° bins), Directional Shannon Entropy quantifies turbulence mathematically.

H = - ∑ [ p_i · log₂(p_i) ] for i=1..8 | H_norm = H / log₂(8) = H / 3.0 ∈ [0.0, 1.0]
🟢 H_norm: 0.00 – 0.35
ORDERED / LAMINAR

Pedestrians marching unidirectionally. Flow is calm, predictable, and safe.

🟡 H_norm: 0.35 – 0.70
CONGESTED / DISORDERED

Multidirectional turns and slowing speed. Crowd seeking alternative exits.

🔴 H_norm: 0.70 – 1.00
PANIC TURBULENCE

Opposing vectors colliding, forming circular vortices. 10-second warning for collapse.

The 4-Factor Heuristic Risk Synthesis Engine

Combining density, velocity, entropy, and acceleration into a calibrated 0–100 score

Risk(t) = 100 · [ w₁ · Density(t) + w₂ · Flow(t) + w₃ · Entropy(t) + w₄ · Surge(t) ]
Weights normalize strictly to 1.0 (w₁=0.35, w₂=0.25, w₃=0.25, w₄=0.15) to guarantee numerical stability.
